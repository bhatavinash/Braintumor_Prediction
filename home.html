BRAIN TUMOR DETECTION

Gliomas

"These are tumors that originate from glial cells, which are the cells that support and nourish the neurons in the brain. Gliomas are the most common type of brain tumor and can be either low-grade (slow-growing) or high-grade (fast-growing)".

Meningiomas

"These are tumors that originate from the meninges, which are the membranes that cover and protect the brain and spinal cord. Meningiomas are usually benign and slow-growing, but they can be dangerous if they grow large enough to put pressure on the brain".

pituitary

"These are tumors that originate from the pituitary gland, which is a small gland located at the base of the brain that produces hormones that regulate various bodily functions. Pituitary tumors can be benign or malignant and can cause a variety of symptoms".
